Zones

The colours here are the ones the panel uses — zone 1 is red, the rest spread evenly round the wheel — so Show zones puts exactly this picture on the rocks. Unpainted pixels stay dark.

Polygon: click the corners, then click the first point again (or Enter) to fill it. Esc cancels.

Rotate turns the view only, for when you are stood behind the panel or it is mounted on its side. What goes on the wire is always panel-native, so the board never has to know.

Live to panel publishes as you draw, so the rock lights up while the brush is still on it. Save is the one that retains the map.

Calibrate lights one block of the panel white and asks which rock it is behind, then steps to the next. The rocks you have already worked out stay lit in their own colours while it asks — what you are checking is this one against those. It walks in a serpentine, so the block after this one is next to it and Enter — same as the last — is usually the answer. Zones touching the block are offered first.

Stop & save ends the walk and retains the map, and Calibrate picks up where you left off next time; Restart goes back to the first block. Progress is also retained every 20 blocks, so a stray refresh cannot cost you an hour of it.

1–9 pick a zone, 0 erases, B brush, P polygon, G grid, [ ] size, R rotate.
