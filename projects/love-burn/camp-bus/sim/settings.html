Sound off

The instruments page synthesises drum hits for the mushrooms and a held pad voice for the geodes so you can hear a route being played without Ableton. Off here means silent everywhere, in every open tab. The real instruments will play through the booth's Ableton rig, not through these pages.

Who is touching the geodes

Capacitive one-wire sensors read how well a body couples to the brass frame: a barefoot adult on damp sand reads far higher than a kid in trainers, and two people holding hands read higher still. The simulator picks the baseline touch strength from this profile (jittered per touch); the bus carries it as velocity plus a live value while held. The real bridge normalises each pad against its own untouched baseline.
