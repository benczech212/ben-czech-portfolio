Camp Bus

Asking the broker how camp is doing…

Virtual camp The crystals, the hallway, the entrance screen and the map. Throw a cast and watch it cross camp. Instruments Play the mushrooms, geodes, plant and water tubes, and set the MIDI table that Ableton follows. Devices Every node on the bus: what is answering, what has gone quiet, and a way into each device's own console. Routes Decide what each input controls. Edits go live in the mapping engine as you save them. GPS Where a node thinks it is, in metres: scatter, drift, and the casts thrown from it. Booth devices Resolume and the kitchen bell, with check-in and food-call buttons to drive them. Settings Test sounds, who is touching the geodes, and which broker this browser talks to. Bus console The plumbing: loaded rules, audio meters, and every message on the wire as it happens.

Broker . Remap the camp on the Routes page or by editing booth/mappings.yaml — the engine reloads it on save and rejects a bad edit rather than taking the camp down. The contract every builder codes against is docs/SCHEMA.md, and handbook/ explains how the whole thing fits together.
