Library names

Checks every video in the NAS library's Movies and TV Shows folders (what the NAS Plex shows) against the naming standard: Movies\Title (Year).mkv and TV Shows\Show (Year)\Season 01\Show (Year) - s01e01 - Title.mkv. It runs every 6 hours while this dashboard is open, and when you press Scan now. The right name comes from the NAS Plex's own match. A file is renamed automatically only when Plex's match and the file name agree; everything else waits here for you. Subtitles and other files with the same name move along, every rename is logged (dashboard/rename_log.csv) and can be undone below, and files the re-encode queue still needs are left alone.

Re-encoding

loading…

Titles you keep that are very large or in a format that doesn't stream well are re-encoded to the standard (HEVC 10-bit in MKV, E-AC3 instead of DTS/TrueHD, at most 1080p and 2.5 GB per hour) and written to their final, properly named place in the NAS library. One title at a time on the GPU (NVENC); each result is checked (length, tracks, a full decode and a VMAF quality score on three samples) before it counts as done. Original sources are never changed by this.

Plex keep / drop

Every title is marked. Switching a title to Drop takes its waiting jobs off the re-encode queue; its files are deleted only when you ask me to. Ticking or unticking "Re-encode" does the same for one title.
